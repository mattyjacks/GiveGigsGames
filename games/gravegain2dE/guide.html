GraveGain2DE: Smash Slash Bash Mash — Guide

Core Combat Rules

  • Damage % & Knockback: Instead of HP bars, every hit raises your opponent's Damage %. Higher % makes them fly farther when launched!
  • Blast Zones & Recovery: Knock rivals off any edge of the screen to score a KO. Use your double jump and Up + Special to recover back to the stage or grab a ledge.
  • Ledge Tech: Snap to glowing yellow stage corners while falling. Press Jump to leap up, Forward to climb, or Attack for a quick ledge strike.
  • Shield & Dodging: Hold Shield to block hits (don't let your bubble break!). Tap Left/Right while shielding to Roll Dodge, Down to Spot Dodge, or press Shield mid-air to Air Dodge.
  • Meteor Spikes: Press Down + Attack in mid-air to slam airborne foes straight downward!

The 16 GraveGain Heroes

Pick any combination of the four MoonRock races (Human, Elf, Dwarf, Orc) and four combat classes (Warrior, Tank, Support, Mage) — including Soldier, Warden, Medic, Engineer, Assassin, Paladin, Brewer, Tinkerer, Slayer, Brute, Shaman, Warlock, Berserker, Guardian, Druid, and Witch.

  • Human: Balanced EVA suit mobility and Orbital SafeSpace Strike.
  • Elf: Fast movement, high jump, and Mother Tree Tempest.
  • Dwarf: Heavy weight (hardest to launch), hard hits, and Sparkite Magma Eruption.
  • Orc: High power with Berserkonin Rage scaling at high %, plus First Orc Cataclysm.

Controls

  • 1P Keyboard: WASD or Arrow Keys to move, W/↑/Space to jump, J/Z Attack, K/X Special, L/C/Shift Shield/Dodge, U/V Charge Smash.
  • 2P Split Keyboard: P1 uses WASD + F/G/H/R; P2 uses Arrows + Numpad 1/2/3/0 (or / . , Shift).
  • Gamepad: Brawl supports up to four local controllers. Switch fighter slots 3 and 4 to GAMEPAD to use controllers 3 and 4. Left stick / D-pad move, face buttons for jump / attack / special / smash, shoulders for shield.
  • Touch (phone / tablet): Left virtual stick to move — flick or hold up to jump (same as keyboard Up). Right-side buttons: JUMP, ATK, SPEC, SHLD, and hold SMASH to charge (aim with the stick, then release). Prefer landscape for the fullest arena view; portrait keeps a dedicated control strip under the playfield.
