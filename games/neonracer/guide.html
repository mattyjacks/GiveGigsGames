🌴 Neon Racer: Miami '86 — Walkthrough

Objective & Synthwave Strips

Tear down a 3D 1986 Miami synthwave causeway at sunset, weaving through retro coupes, boxy vans, and telegraphing lane-swapping rivals across four escalating strips: Ocean Dr, Biscayne Bay (500m), Vice Overdrive (1200m), and Midnight 86 (2200m).

  • Near-Miss Combos: Thread closely past cars in adjacent lanes to chain a x2–x5 Streak Multiplier for +15m × combo bonuses. At x4+, road coins pay double!
  • Road Features (380m+): Hit glowing cyan >>> Speed Pads for an instant turbo burst and +25m. Avoid iridescent Oil Slicks that briefly wobble steering and cut speed.
  • Lane-Swappers (350m+): Watch for flashing amber blinkers and floating ◀/▶ arrows before rivals glide into an adjacent lane.

Timed Power-Ups & Miami Garage

🛡️ Neon ShieldWraps your supercar in an energy dome that absorbs 1 crash, blasting the rival flying for +100m.
🧲 Coin MagnetPulls nearby coins from every lane into your car for 8s (11s with Hyper Magnet).
⚡ Turbo Overdrive5s high-speed warp with wider FOV, speedlines, and 1.5× distance gain.
🐢 Chill Wave6s synthwave time-dilation that slows traffic for surgical weaving.

Coins collected each run bank into the Miami Garage to unlock 5 iconic 80s 3D supercars (Testa Cyan, Vice White, OutRun Spyder, Flux DMC-86, and Solar Countach) plus permanent Starter Shield and Hyper Magnet upgrades.

Controls & Saves

  • Keyboard: ←/→ or A/D switch lanes; ↑/↓ or W/S accelerate/brake; C switch camera (Chase / Hood / Arcade); Space start/restart; P/Esc pause; M mute synth audio; F fullscreen.
  • Touch: Tap left/right halves or swipe lanes, use on-screen ◀ ▶ ▼ ▲ GAS pedals, or toggle TILT gyroscope steering with ZERO calibration.
  • Saves: Best distance saves in localStorage (neonRacerHighScore) and Garage unlocks/coins save in neonRacerGarageV1.
